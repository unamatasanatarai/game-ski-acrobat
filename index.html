PHYSICS & SIMULATION

Built Like an Olympic Event

Every element of Freestyle Aerials is grounded in deterministic aerial mechanics and official competition rules.

⏱️

120Hz Decoupled Physics

The simulation steps at a strict 120Hz fixed-timestep accumulator, ensuring consistent rotational velocity and flight arcs across 60Hz, 120Hz, and 144Hz displays.

🔄

Multi-Axis Stunt Engine

Execute continuous 360° backflips and frontflips, combine them with axial pirouette spins, or hold classic grabs like the Daffy Split, Mule Kick, and Swan Dive.

🎯

Slope Alignment Meter

A live HUD needle gauges your angular deviation from the 34.7° snow hill. Center the needle in the green zone and release trick holds before touchdown for a Perfect Landing.

🏅

5-Judge Olympic Scoring

Judges from USA, Sweden, Switzerland, Austria, and Japan grade your jump. High and low scores are discarded, trick repetitions decay, and scores tally to 10.00.

📋 FIS JUDGING CRITERIA

  • 1
    Difficulty & Multipliers: Frontflips (2.6 pts), Backflips (2.4 pts), Twists (1.8 pts), Swan Dive (1.5 pts), Daffy & Mule Kick (1.3 pts).
  • 2
    Diminishing Returns: Repeating the same trick reduces score value (100% on 1st, 80% on 2nd, 60% on 3rd). Variety is rewarded!
  • 3
    Landing Bonus: Perfect alignment adds +2.8 points. Clean landing adds +1.4 points.
  • 4
    Crash Penalty: Touching down with an active stunt, improper angle, or unresolved twist causes a crash and immediate Disqualification (0.00).
  • 5
    Olympic Trim: High and low scores from the 5 judges are dropped to eliminate individual bias.
